What changed

  • Tiny represented lengths stay attached to one material parcel. Numerical residuals no longer become separate beds with separate compaction histories.
  • Deposition, subsidence, normal-fault transport, and raster cuts use shared exact binary boundaries, preserving thin material and complementary intervals.
  • Retained material follows subsidence at its remembered physical horizontal position.
  • Thrust matching rejects slightly overfull placements. An infeasible whole-packet placement can split material conservatively within the existing local stencil, retaining each child's flow moment.

What remains unresolved

Grid sensitivity remains in coupled deformation. Exact bookkeeping prevents numerical loss; it does not provide exact physical footprints or remove sensitivity to where real material is split into parcels. The 10 m grid is a comparison level, not ground truth.

Across all shared profiles, coarse travel-time disagreement falls about 5.7%, but fine disagreement rises about 38.0%. Aggregate thickness agreement is nearly unchanged. Coupled B worsens in coarse thickness and both travel-time comparisons. Coupled C fine-thickness disagreement increases about 42.5%. Coupled A unit 10 at x = 9,200 m also has a larger composition mismatch: its 20 m sand fraction changes from about 0.30793 to 0.35700 while its 40 m and 10 m values stay near 0.307. These are open regressions.

The saved records do not fully resolve excluded thin/fragmented material, basement quadrature, and velocity-projection contributions. Neither the 20 m nor 10 m run used the capacity fallback.

Fault classification, boundary continuation, and deposition accommodation still use stored column coordinates. The fallback allocates capacity around parent targets; it is not a general transport method based on exact polygon overlap. Physical constitutive calculations and grain totals remain floating-point calculations.

Next implementation target — not yet validated

Use one physical-coordinate contract for fault cuts, available destination space, the sediment surface, new deposition, incoming boundary material, and subsequent compaction and fault-marker updates. Test that changing storage coordinates while retaining the same physical material leaves the result invariant. Analytical planar faults, affine boundary continuation, known deposition contacts, and constant-porosity loading provide independent checks. If translated material footprints overlap or cannot be represented conservatively, report that failure explicitly before choosing a broader geometric transport method. The unchanged development comparison should follow these checks.

Verification and reproducibility

All 4,494 operation budgets pass an independent audit of 755,160 scalar, component, and bed balances. Maximum absolute residual: 3.33413e-06 m²; worst residual/tolerance ratio: 0.00190197. Tolerance remains 10⁻⁶ m² + 10⁻¹⁰ × inventory scale. Event inventories are continuous and no sediment repairs were added.

All 26 test files were run in three disjoint batches against one frozen source snapshot; type checking and the build pass. One existing fixture now constructs exact complementary intervals and compares exact support. Its original numerical tolerances and behavior checks are retained. New tests include independent known answers, repeated lifecycle checks, sub-cell beds, and capacity infeasibility. Selected capacity and child-flow tests were also checked against deliberately broken variants.

The same twelve cases, seeds, prescribed histories, three grids, measurement code, eligibility rules, and conservation gates were retained. Neither the observational development arrays nor the holdout arrays or split files were read or changed.

Memory screening: the compression-C 10 m candidate completed in 155.71 seconds, with process peak RSS about 4.67 GiB under an 8 GiB Node heap limit. Other tests overlapped this run, so the timing is not a controlled speed comparison. This measures a numerical batch run, not browser responsiveness. A separate C10 run in a fresh process also completed with a 4 GiB old-space limit, in 151.07 seconds with 2.82 GiB peak RSS. Its samples and budgets are byte-identical to the earlier 8 GiB screen, and its budget matches the formal study. This single-case check does not establish that the whole suite or browser replay fits that limit.
